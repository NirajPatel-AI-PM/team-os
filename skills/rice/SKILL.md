# RICE

Score each candidate against the outcome the team owns, not against the person asking.

| Factor | Meaning | Scale |
|---|---|---|
| Reach | People affected per quarter | A count, from data where possible |
| Impact | Effect on the outcome for each of them | 3 massive, 2 high, 1 medium, 0.5 low, 0.25 minimal |
| Confidence | How sure we are of reach and impact | 100%, 80%, 50%. Below 50% means discovery first |
| Effort | Person-months | A number |

Score = Reach × Impact × Confidence ÷ Effort.

Then write, for every item that does not make the cut:

1. The score and the item it lost to.
2. What would change the answer: new evidence, lower effort, or a shift in the outcome.
3. How the requester can reach their outcome without the feature, if there is a way. There often is.

Share the whole table, not only the verdict. A visible ranking is easier to accept than a private one.
